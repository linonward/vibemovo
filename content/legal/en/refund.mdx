This Refund Policy explains when <ProjectName /> issues refunds.

## Failed generations

If an image or video fails, or doesn't finish within 24 hours, we return its credits to your balance automatically. You don't need to contact us.

## Credit packs

- You can get a full refund within **14 days** of purchase if you haven't used any credits from the pack.
- After you use credits from a pack, or after 14 days, the purchase is not refundable, unless the law requires otherwise.

## Subscriptions

- You can cancel at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you paid for, and you keep your credits.
- You can get a full refund of a charge within **14 days** if you haven't used any credits from that charge.
- We don't give partial refunds for unused time.

## Incorrect charges

If you were charged twice or charged the wrong amount, we refund the extra charge in full.

## How to request a refund

Email <SupportEmail /> from the email address on your account. Include your order number and the reason for the request. We reply by email.

## How refunds work

- Waffo Pancake, our Merchant of Record, processes refunds.
- Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank may take several business days to show them.
- When we refund a purchase, we remove its credits from your balance. If you have already spent some of them, we remove only what is left. Your balance never goes below zero.

## Not refundable

- Purchases outside the conditions above, unless the law requires otherwise.
- Credits on accounts that we close for breaking our Terms of Service.

This policy doesn't limit any rights you have under the consumer protection laws where you live.
