The zombie couple video, also called the "zombie love story", is a short AI clip that keeps going around TikTok, Reels and Shorts. One person survives the apocalypse. Their partner walks out of the fog as a zombie. The survivor raises a weapon, recognizes them, lowers it and hugs them anyway. Then the clip cuts to a happy memory of the two before it all happened.

The [Zombie couple](/en/ai-zombie-couple-video) page makes that clip from two photos: one of you and one of your partner. You get a 15-second vertical video with music and sound. In our test, it was ready in 3 to 5 minutes.

## How it works

1. Open **Video generator → Zombie couple** in the dashboard.
2. Upload **your photo**. You play the survivor.
3. Upload **your partner's photo**. Your partner turns into the zombie.
4. Select **Generate (75 credits)**.
5. Wait on the page, or leave it. The video appears in **Your videos** when it is ready, and you get a notification. If generation fails, the credits come back.

Each photo is a JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 2 MB. Behind the page, Alibaba's **Wan 3.0** reference-to-video model gets both photos in one request, with a prompt that tells the whole story in three beats:

| Seconds | What happens |
| ------- | ------------ |
| 0–6     | A ruined city street at dusk. The survivor holds a bat. The partner staggers out of the fog as a zombie. |
| 6–11    | The survivor recognizes them, lowers the bat and hugs them. The zombie calms down. |
| 11–15   | A match cut on the hug, to the same couple in a sunny kitchen, both human and laughing. |

## What a real result looks like

We gave the page two photos of AI-generated people: a woman in a green suit as the survivor, and a man in a blue suit as the partner. These four frames are taken at 3.5, 6.5, 11 and 14 seconds of the result:

- **The story played in order.** The zombie came out of the fog at about 2 seconds, the bat went down by 6.5, the hug started by 9, and the cut to the kitchen memory came at about 12.
- **Both people stayed recognizable.** The zombie kept the partner's face, hair and blue suit, so the hug reads as a reunion, not a stranger.
- **The outfits stayed the same in the street.** The green suit, the white shirt and the blue suit kept their colors and cut.
- **The memory changed one detail.** In the kitchen, the survivor wears the white shirt without the green jacket.
- **The zombie had some red marks.** The prompt asks for film makeup with no blood, and the face and jacket still got a few red patches. They look like stage makeup, not wounds.
- **The video is ready to post.** It is a 720×1280 MP4, 15 seconds, with sound.

## Choose photos that work

- **One person per photo.** Two faces in one photo can end up mixed.
- **Face the camera, in good light.** The model copies the face it can see, and the zombie still has to look like your partner.
- **Show more of the body if you can.** A half-body or full-body photo keeps the real outfit. A close-up leaves the rest to the model.
- **Put the photos in the right order.** The first photo is the survivor. The second photo becomes the zombie.
- **Use photos you may use.** Only animate people who agreed to it.

## Questions

**How long does it take?** A few minutes. Ours took 3 to 5. You can leave the page; the video waits in **All videos**.

**What does it cost?** 75 credits for one 15-second video. A failed video is refunded. See the [pricing page](/en/pricing) for credit packs.

**Can both of us turn into zombies?** Not on this page. The second photo is always the zombie, and the first photo always survives.

**Is it gory?** No. The prompt asks for film-makeup zombies with no blood, wounds or biting. Some red marks can still appear, as in our test.

**Can I choose the music?** Not yet. The model creates the music and the sound effects. You can mute the clip and add a sound in your editing app.

**What format is the video?** A 9:16 MP4, 720×1280, 15 seconds, with sound. You can post it to TikTok, Reels or Shorts without cropping.
