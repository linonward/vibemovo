You need one photo and one sentence. The [Video generator](/en/videos) uses the photo as the first frame and the sentence as the motion: a camera push-in, wind in the hair, a person who turns and smiles. The video is ready in 1 to 5 minutes, and you can play or download it on the same page.

## How it works

1. Open **Video generator** in the dashboard.
2. Upload a **start image**: a JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 4 MB. The video starts from this image.
3. Write the **prompt**: what moves, and how. Or select an example under the prompt box.
4. Select the **length**: 5 or 10 seconds.
5. Select the **resolution**: 720p or 1080p.
6. Select **Generate**. The button shows the price in credits before you spend anything.
7. Wait on the page or leave it. The video appears in **Your videos** when it is ready. If generation fails, the credits come back.

No photo yet? Make one in the **Image generator** first, download it, and upload it as the start image.

## Write a prompt that moves

The photo already sets the scene, so the prompt only has to say what changes. Short and specific works best.

- **Name one main motion.** "The person turns toward the camera and smiles" is better than a list of five actions.
- **Say what the camera does.** "The camera slowly pushes in", "the camera orbits slowly around the subject" or "static camera".
- **Say what stays still.** "A light wind moves the hair and leaves. The subject stays still" keeps the face steady.
- **Describe motion, not the picture.** You don't need to repeat the colors or the clothes; the model sees them.
- **Keep it possible in 5 seconds.** A long story won't fit. Pick the 10-second length for slower or larger moves.

The examples under the prompt box are a good start: **Push in**, **Wind**, **Turn and smile**, **Time-lapse** and **Orbit**. Select one, then change the words to fit your photo.

## Choose a photo that works

- **Use a clear, sharp image.** Blur and heavy compression get worse when they move.
- **Leave room for the motion.** A face cut off at the edge has nowhere to turn.
- **Check the size.** Each side must be between 240 and 8,000 pixels, and the long side can be at most 8 times the short side. The page checks this before you spend credits.
- **Use photos you may use.** Only animate people who agreed to it.

Photos and prompts go through content moderation. If a start image or prompt is blocked, you keep your credits.

## What it costs

Videos cost credits per second, and the price depends on the resolution:

| Resolution | Per second | 5 seconds | 10 seconds |
| ---------- | ---------- | --------- | ---------- |
| 720p       | 5 credits  | 25        | 50         |
| 1080p      | 8 credits  | 40        | 80         |

A failed video is refunded. See [Pricing](/en/pricing) for credit packs and plans.

## Questions

**How long does it take?** Usually 1 to 5 minutes. You can leave the page; the video waits in **All videos**.

**Can I make a video from text only?** No. The Video generator needs a start image. Make one with the Image generator first.

**What happens to my photo?** The start image is deleted a day after upload. The video stays in your library.

**Can I make two people rap?** Yes, with the [Rap duo](/en/blog/ai-rap-video-two-people). It takes two photos and two names instead of a prompt.
