## Make a Migos AI video from two photos

1. Open **Video generator → Rap duo** in the dashboard.
2. Upload the **left photo** and the **right photo**, one person in each.
3. Type the **left name** and the **right name**.
4. Select **Generate (75 credits)**.
5. Wait for the video. It appears in **Your videos**, where you can play or download it.

## It's not the real Migos song

The Rap duo doesn't use "Hotel Lobby" or any other Migos song, and it doesn't copy anyone's voice. It writes new lines from the names you type and creates its own beat and vocals. That matters when you post: a clip that uses a copyrighted song can be muted or taken down.

## Tips for a better clip

- **Use clear, front-facing photos** in good light. The model copies the face it can see.
- **Show more of the body** if you can. A full-body photo keeps the real outfit. A portrait leaves the rest to the model.
- **Crop for vertical feeds.** The video is 16:9. For TikTok or Reels, crop it to 9:16.
- **Use photos you may use.** Only animate people who agreed to it.
