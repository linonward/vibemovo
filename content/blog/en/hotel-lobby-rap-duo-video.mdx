The hotel lobby rap duo is a viral two-person clip: two people in an orange booth, one microphone hanging between them, trading lines. The [Rap duo](/en/ai-rap-video-generator) makes that kind of clip from two photos and two names, and it makes the song too.

## Song and video in one step

Many rap duo AI tools give you a silent clip, and you add a sound on TikTok. The Rap duo gives you a finished clip with sound:

- **The song:** two rhyming lines written for the two names, one line each, with a beat and vocals.
- **The video:** both people in an orange studio with a hanging microphone. The left person raps first, then the right person takes the mic.
- **The lip sync:** each person's mouth moves with their own line.

The result is a 15-second 720p MP4 with sound, ready to download.

## Make a hotel lobby rap duo video from two photos

1. Open **Video generator → Rap duo** in the dashboard.
2. Upload the **left photo** and the **right photo**, one person in each.
3. Type the **left name** and the **right name**.
4. Select **Generate (75 credits)**.
5. Wait for the video. It appears in **Your videos**, where you can play or download it.

In our test, the video was ready in about five minutes. If generation fails, the credits come back. For photo limits, a real test run and what the model gets right and wrong, read [How to Make an AI Rap Video of Two People from Photos](/en/blog/ai-rap-video-two-people).

## Your own song, not a copy

The Rap duo doesn't use an existing song, and it doesn't copy any artist's voice. It writes new lines from the names you type and creates its own beat and vocals. That matters when you post: a clip that uses a copyrighted song can be muted or taken down.

## Tips for a better clip

- **Use clear, front-facing photos** in good light. The model copies the face it can see.
- **Show more of the body** if you can. A full-body photo keeps the real outfit. A portrait leaves the rest to the model.
- **Crop for vertical feeds.** The video is 16:9. For TikTok or Reels, crop it to 9:16.
- **Use photos you may use.** Only animate people who agreed to it.
