"Hug your younger self" started as an AI photo edit: a photo of you today and one of you as a child, merged into one embrace. The video version goes a step further. You walk up to your younger self, kneel down and hold them, and the moment plays out with music.

The [younger-self hug](/en/hug-your-younger-self-ai) page makes that video from two photos. You get a 15-second vertical clip with music and sound. In our test, it was ready about 12 minutes after we submitted it.

## How it works

1. Open **Video generator → Hug your younger self** in the dashboard.
2. Upload **your photo today**. You play the adult.
3. Upload **your childhood photo**. The child in the video keeps that face.
4. Select **Generate (75 credits)**.
5. Wait on the page, or leave it. The video appears in **Your videos** when it is ready, and you get a notification. If generation fails, the credits come back.

Each photo is a JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 2 MB. Behind the page, Alibaba's **Wan 3.0** reference-to-video model gets both photos in one request, with a prompt in three beats:

| Seconds | What the prompt asks for |
| ------- | ------------------------ |
| 0–5     | A sunny park in the late afternoon. The child plays alone on the grass. The adult walks in and stops, surprised. |
| 5–11    | The child looks up and recognizes them. The adult kneels down and they hug, eyes closed, smiling. |
| 11–15   | The camera rises. The child dissolves into golden light and drifts up with the dandelion seeds. |

## What a real result looks like

We gave the page two photos of the same AI-generated person: a man in a blue suit today, and the same man as a boy in a striped shirt and sunglasses. These four frames are taken at 2, 6.5, 9.5 and 12.5 seconds of the result:

![Four frames from a 15-second AI younger-self hug video: a man in a blue suit walks into a sunny park toward a boy playing with a toy car, kneels beside him, hugs him with his eyes closed, then golden light surrounds the boy.](/landing/hug/frames.jpg)

- **The first two beats played as asked.** The man walked in while the boy played with a toy car, knelt beside him by about 6 seconds, and the boy got up and hugged him at about 8.
- **Both stayed recognizable.** The man kept his face and blue suit. The boy kept the face, the hair, the striped shirt and the sunglasses from the childhood photo.
- **The ending was softer than the prompt.** At about 12 seconds, golden light surrounded the boy. He didn't dissolve: at the end, both of them knelt in the grass and looked up at the light.
- **The video had one dissolve.** At about 4 seconds, the wide shot crossfaded into a closer one.
- **The video is ready to post.** It is a 720×1280 MP4, 15 seconds, with sound.

## Choose photos that work

- **One person per photo.** Two faces in one photo can end up mixed.
- **Show the face clearly in both photos.** The model copies the faces it can see, so the child should look like a younger you.
- **Show more of the body if you can.** A half-body or full-body photo keeps the real outfit. A close-up leaves the rest to the model.
- **Put the photos in the right order.** The first photo is you today. The second photo is you as a child.
- **Use photos you may use.** Only animate people who agreed to it.

## Questions

**How long does it take?** A few minutes to about a quarter of an hour. Ours took about 12. You can leave the page; the video waits in **All videos**.

**What does it cost?** 75 credits for one 15-second video. A failed video is refunded. See the [pricing page](/en/pricing) for credit packs.

**Does the child always disappear at the end?** No. The prompt asks for it, and in our test the child glowed but stayed. Your result can differ.

**Can I change the place?** Not on this page. The scene is always a sunny park.

**What format is the video?** A 9:16 MP4, 720×1280, 15 seconds, with sound. You can post it to TikTok, Reels or Shorts without cropping.
